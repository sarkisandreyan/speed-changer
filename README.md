# Speed Changer

[![Download on the Chrome Web Store](images/Download-on-the-Chrome-Web-Store.svg)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/speed-changer/albfdjflebpipkmmpjblndlcjljidlei)
[![Open Interactive Demo](images/Open-Interactive-Demo.svg)](https://speed-changer.andreyan.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=sc_repo&utm_content=badge)

A powerful cross-browser extension that allows you to play media up to 16x faster or slower using on-page controls. It brings the familiar experience from the apps you use to your browser — press and hold the floating buttons to play at 2x, pull away while pressing to lock the speed.

Not a fan of the on-page floating buttons? Speed Changer also features a more traditional pop-up experience.

Check out this page for an interactive demo — [Speed Changer](https://speed-changer.andreyan.com).

## Features

- 🚀 CONTROL WITHOUT LIMITS — Bypass platform limitations and play media content up to 16x faster or slower.
- ▶️ CHANGE THE SPEED DIRECTLY FROM THE PAGE — No need to bounce between the page and the browser toolbar anymore — the on-page floating buttons make changing the speed a breeze.
- 🍃 FRIENDLY AND FAMILIAR EXPERIENCE — All the familiar gestures from the apps you use brought to your browser — press and hold the floating buttons to play at 2x, pull away while pressing to lock the speed.
- 💬 TRADITIONAL POP-UP INTERFACE — Not a fan of the on-page floating buttons? Just turn them off and use the more traditional pop-up experience provided by the extension.
- ⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS — Use your keyboard to quickly change the playback speed — press Alt/⌥+2 to play at 2x, Alt/⌥+3 to play at 3x etc., or use your own shortcuts.
- 🎵 AUDIO CONTENT SUPPORT — Listen to your favorite podcasts at your desired speed, or create sped-up versions of your favorite songs on the spot.
- 🖤 DARK MODE FRIENDLY — The extension adapts to the color scheme of your browser so you will not strain your eyes at nighttime.
- 🔧 HIGHLY CUSTOMIZABLE — Make yourself at home with the extension by adjusting the wide range of configuration options to your preferences.
